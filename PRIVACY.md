# Privacy Policy

Applies to tail for Windows (tail-win), including the Microsoft Store package.
Last updated: 2026-10-05.

tail for Windows does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

* It runs entirely on your computer and makes no network connections.
* It reads only the files, and the standard input, that you name on the
  command line, and writes their content to the console or to where you
  redirect it. It does not modify or keep copies of them.
* It contains no telemetry, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
* It creates no accounts and stores no settings.

Microsoft may collect data about the installation of Store apps under its own
privacy statement; the author has no access to it beyond the aggregate
statistics Partner Center shows to every publisher.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/badbat75/tail.win64/issues>.
